# OlympiadPDFs — Complete Website Specification

## 1. Website Overview

**OlympiadPDFs** is a modern Indian EdTech e-commerce platform that sells **expert-designed Olympiad practice/sample question papers in PDF format** for students of **Classes 6 to 10**.

The website is designed around one simple customer journey:

> **Discover → Explore → Purchase → Pay → Receive PDF → Practice**

The website should be **clean, trustworthy, affordable, student-friendly, and highly conversion-focused**, while also looking professional enough for parents to trust the product.

### Brand

**OlympiadPDFs**

### Tagline

**Expert Practice. Better Preparation.**

### Brand colors

* **Primary:** Deep/Royal Blue
* **Secondary:** White
* **Accent:** Yellow/Gold

The visual language should consistently use these three colors across the website, PDF covers, social media, advertisements, buttons, icons, and promotional material.

---

# 2. Main Business Model

OlympiadPDFs is a **digital-product business**.

There is no physical delivery.

A customer:

1. Visits the website.
2. Selects their class.
3. Selects a subject or bundle.
4. Views the available practice papers.
5. Applies a coupon if available.
6. Pays using Razorpay.
7. Payment is verified automatically.
8. The purchased PDF is automatically delivered to their email.
9. The customer downloads the PDF and starts practicing.

### Complete flow

```text
Instagram / Facebook / Meta Ads
              ↓
        OlympiadPDFs
           Website
              ↓
       Select Class
              ↓
      Select Subject
              ↓
      View Product
              ↓
          Buy Now
              ↓
        Apply Coupon
              ↓
      Razorpay Checkout
              ↓
      Payment Verification
              ↓
       Order Confirmation
              ↓
      Automatic PDF Email
              ↓
        Download PDF
              ↓
       Start Practicing
```

There should be **no manual PDF delivery** after launch.

# 17. Important Content Number Issue

The original requirement says:

> 60 papers × 35 questions

which equals:

**2,100 questions**

But another requirement says:

> 4,900 questions = 140 × 35

Therefore, the final website should **not hardcode 4,900 questions** until the content team confirms the actual number.

The product system should allow:

* Paper count
* Question count
* Class
* Subject

to be configured dynamically.

# 54. Final Product Vision

**OlympiadPDFs is not simply a website where PDFs are uploaded.**

It should be built as a **complete digital commerce system for academic practice material**.

The first version should establish three things:

### 1. Brand

A trustworthy identity for affordable Olympiad preparation.

### 2. Product

High-quality, expert-designed practice papers.

### 3. Automated Commerce

A customer can purchase and receive the product without human intervention.

The most important V1 success scenario is:

> **A parent sees an Instagram/Facebook advertisement → visits OlympiadPDFs on their phone → selects Class 6 Science → sees a professional product card → applies a social-media coupon → pays ₹99 through Razorpay → receives the correct PDF automatically by email within minutes.**
